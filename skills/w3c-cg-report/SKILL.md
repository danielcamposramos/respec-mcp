# Community Group reports

Use CG-DRAFT or CG-FINAL only when that is the report's actual status; an individual submission hosted by a group may remain unofficial. Include the group identity and public link, date and accurate status statement. Keep contribution history traceable. Let the generator supply the appropriate boilerplate rather than inventing stronger claims of endorsement.

Check the repository's profile, then preflight and render. Phrase bans are literal editorial rules and can flag quotations or disclaimers; inspect the finding rather than treating it as proof of a misleading claim. Final-report commitments and the group's publication decision require their own evidence. Do not infer them from a passing check or a file called final.

## Shared guidance and tools

Read [Shared editorial principles](../../docs/EDITORIAL_PRINCIPLES.md) for native-language collaboration and the boundaries of automated checks. Use only tools applicable to this document: ReSpec CG-DRAFT / CG-FINAL, respec-check, W3C CG report publication tools.
