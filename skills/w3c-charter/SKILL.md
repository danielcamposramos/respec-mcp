# Working and Interest Group charters

Start from the current charter template linked by the guide. Make the mission, scope and exclusions, deliverables, dependencies, participation and decision arrangements concrete. Separate confirmed support from organizations merely contacted. For work originating in a CG, identify the proposed deliverables and ongoing relationship.

Track substantive changes and unresolved scope disagreements. The charter process includes human refinement, review and decisions; generating a charter does not establish a group or authorize its work. Use the template's own HTML conventions and available checks. ReSpec validation is applicable only if this charter is actually authored in ReSpec, not as a blanket charter compliance check.

## Shared guidance and tools
