# Agendas and meeting minutes

For agendas, identify the date, time zone, participation details and topics with the decisions or discussion needed. For minutes, use the actual transcript or scribe record to distinguish discussion, proposals, resolutions, actions and unresolved questions. Preserve attribution where supported; flag uncertain transcription.

Use the group's established AgendaBot, Zakim, RRSAgent and scribe2 workflow where available. Verify generated minutes against the record before representing them as final. A transcript is not automatically an agreed resolution. These tools are distinct from ReSpec; the respec-mcp validator does not validate attendance, consensus or the truth of meeting records.

## Shared guidance and tools

Read [Shared editorial principles](../../docs/EDITORIAL_PRINCIPLES.md) for native-language collaboration and the boundaries of automated checks. Use only tools applicable to this document: AgendaBot, Zakim, RRSAgent, scribe2.
