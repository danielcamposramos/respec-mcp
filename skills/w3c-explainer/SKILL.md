# Explainers

Start from the user's problem and a concrete scenario. Distinguish goals from exclusions, explain the proposed approach with a small example, and compare alternatives in terms of user outcomes. Identify discussion venues and unresolved design questions. Include accessibility, internationalization, privacy and security implications relevant to the proposal; link detailed self-reviews where available.

Use the repository's existing format. For ReSpec HTML, the example-explainer profile checks a structural subset of the guide. It cannot assess whether the rationale, alternatives or stakeholder evidence are convincing. Preserve an individual proposal's status; interest from a participant is not group endorsement. Prepare TAG review material through the review-request skill when requested.
