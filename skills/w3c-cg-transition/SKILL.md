# Community Group transition proposals

Describe the proposed target Working Group and whether its charter covers the work. Identify the deliverables proposed for transfer and the continuing role of the Community Group. Link recorded CG support, actual implementation evidence, unresolved design issues and relevant review history.

Keep readiness evidence separate from future plans. Record the status of contributor commitments through the appropriate W3C process; do not infer those commitments from repository access, authorship or a code merge. A transition proposal remains a proposal until the responsible groups make their decisions. Use the charter skill if a new or revised charter is needed.
